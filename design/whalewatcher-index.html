How the Sonar Reads

Methodology
01

Signal is skill

A contact's signal strength is its Skill Score (0–10): how reliably entry prices beat the market's eventual resolution, Bayesian-shrunk so a few lucky pings can't fake a strong return.

02

Edge, not winnings

We score the per-position mean of resolution − entry across resolved markets — pure foresight. A whale can bleed money on size and still read the future cleanly.

03

Bots never surface

Suspected automata, sub-threshold wallets, and sub-cent longshot farmers are filtered before ranking. Only genuine forecasting contacts make the board.
