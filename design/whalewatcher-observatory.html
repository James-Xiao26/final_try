Observer's Notes✦

Magnitude is skill

A body's apparent magnitude is its Skill Score (0–10): how reliably entry prices anticipate a market's resolution, Bayesian-shrunk so a brief flash of luck cannot pass for a fixed star.

Spectral shift

Shift is edge per share — the mean of resolution − entry over resolved markets, in cents. A blueshift is positive edge approaching; a redshift, edge receding.

Charting the field

Class follows magnitude — O supergiants (≥9) down to M dwarfs. Suspected automata and sub-threshold bodies are never admitted to the register.
