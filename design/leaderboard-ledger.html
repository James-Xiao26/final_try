Methodology❦

How the score is built

Skill Score is pure forecasting edge — how reliably a trader's entry prices beat the market's eventual resolution. It is Bayesian-shrunk toward zero for small samples, so a handful of lucky bets cannot mint a ten.

Edge per share

The per-position mean of resolution − entry price over resolved markets, in cents. A trader who buys YES at 38¢ on outcomes that resolve true earns +62¢ of edge on those shares.

Who is excluded

Suspected bots, wallets below the minimum trade and volume gates, and sub-cent longshot farmers are struck from the ledger before ranking.
